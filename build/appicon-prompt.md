# SilentOpen 应用图标

由内置 image_gen 生成。原始 PNG 保存为 `build/appicon.png`，前端使用同一资产的副本 `frontend/src/assets/appicon.png`。

生成提示词：

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a production desktop application icon for SilentOpen, a local process and listening-port inspector. Generate one original polished square 1024 x 1024 icon with genuine transparent alpha outside the icon. A softly rounded charcoal square tile fills about 88 percent of the canvas. Center a bold, simple ivory open-circle port symbol with three short clean horizontal process-list strokes and a single small muted emerald green status dot. The palette follows a charcoal and white desktop utility with a green running-status indicator. Front-facing, flat graphic construction with very restrained soft depth, balanced generous spacing, clean edges, strong silhouette legible at 24px. No text, no letters, no numerals, no watermark, no Wails branding, no mockup, no perspective, no additional objects, no multiple variants. Deliver the standalone app icon asset only.
